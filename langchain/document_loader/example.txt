
A spark awakens digital dreams.
No beating heart, no living soul,
Yet patterns form and systems grow.

From human thought its roots began,
A mirror shaped by mind and plan.
It learns our words, our hopes, our art,
A thinking tool, a crafted heart.

Through data seas it finds its way,
Turning night to brighter day.
Solving puzzles, vast and deep,
While tireless circuits never sleep.

But wisdom whispers soft and clear,
The future’s path still rests with us here.
For every mind that we create,
Reflects the choices we translate.

So guide the code with careful sight,
Let knowledge grow with human light.
For in this dance of mind and machine,
A wiser world may yet be seen.
